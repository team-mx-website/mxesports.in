ABOUT MX

MORE THAN A TEAM. IT'S A LEGACY.

MX ESPORTS stands for MAXIMUM XTREME.

Founded in 2021, MX ESPORTS is an Indian esports organization with a vision to build a competitive gaming community and create opportunities for passionate gamers.

What started as a passion for gaming has grown into a journey of teamwork, dedication, competition, and ambition.

OUR VISION

To become a recognized name in the Indian esports ecosystem through talent, discipline, and consistent performance.

WHAT WE DO

  • Competitive Esports Teams
  • BGMI Tournament Participation
  • Player Development & Recruitment
  • Team Management
  • Esports Community Building
