ABOUT MX

MORE THAN A TEAM. IT'S A LEGACY.

MX ESPORTS stands for MAXIMUM XTREME. Founded in 2021 | Maximum Xtreme MX ESPORTS is an Indian esports organization founded in 2021 with a vision to build a competitive gaming community and create opportunities for passionate gamers. What started as a passion for gaming has grown into a journey of teamwork, dedication, competition, and ambition. Our mission is to develop talented players, compete in esports tournaments, build strong teams, and represent our organization with pride.

OUR VISION To become a recognized name in the Indian esports ecosystem through talent, discipline, and consistent performance. WHAT WE DO - Competitive Esports Teams - BGMI Tournament Participation - Player Development & Recruitment - Team Management - Esports Community Building OUR IDENTITY MX = MAXIMUM XTREME We believe in pushing limits, building champions, and creating our own legacy. EST. 2021 — THE JOURNEY CONTINUES. Play Hard. Stay United. Dominate Together.
